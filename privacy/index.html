Privacy Policy

OCD: Off, Closed, Done · Last updated 20 October 2026

OCD: Off, Closed, Done is a simple checklist app. This page explains what it does and doesn't do with your information.

Your lists stay on your device

OCD has no accounts and no server. Everything you type (list names, items, custom items, which items you've checked) is saved only in your browser's storage on your own device, or inside the app on your phone. We cannot see it, and it is never uploaded anywhere.

If you delete the app, clear your browser data, or switch devices, your lists are not transferred unless you use the Export and Import buttons, which save a file you control.

Note for Safari users: Safari may delete a website's saved data after 7 days without a visit. Use Export to keep a backup, or add OCD to your home screen.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand how OCD is used, we count a small number of events, for example "a list was created" or "an item was checked". These events include counts only (such as how many items a list has). They never include the names of your lists or items, or anything you type.

For this we use PostHog, an analytics service (PostHog Inc., data stored in the United States). PostHog records a random identifier stored in your browser's local storage, the type of device and browser, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. We do not use cookies, we do not record your screen, and we do not track you across other websites. You can block this with any content blocker; OCD works the same without it.

Hosting

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log the IP address of visitors. See GitHub's privacy statement.

What we don't do

We do not sell or share your information. We do not show ads. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

Children

OCD is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
